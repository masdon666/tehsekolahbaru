# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary users are owners and employees of small food and drink businesses. Owners manage menus, staff, money, and reports. Employees use a mobile-friendly cashier workflow to record sales and permitted expenses.

## Product Purpose

Catatan Usaha helps small business teams record daily activity without requiring accounting or technical knowledge. Success means an owner can understand business performance and staff can complete their authorized tasks quickly and accurately.

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, and clear. The interface should feel dependable and approachable, with plain Indonesian labels and actionable feedback.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded dashboards, unnecessary accounting jargon, and destructive actions without confirmation.

## Design Principles

- Make common tasks understandable without accounting knowledge.
- Keep employee workflows focused and role-appropriate.
- Make financial totals traceable to their underlying transactions.
- Protect business and employee data at both interface and database layers.
- Give clear confirmation and recovery guidance for important actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal conformance target has been specified. Follow general accessibility practices: readable contrast and type, keyboard access, visible focus, descriptive labels, and reduced-motion respect.
